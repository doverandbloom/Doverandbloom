U-Pick Flower Tickets

Wander the rows and fill a mason jar with your own bouquet, right alongside the bees. U-pick runs every other weekend, Saturdays and Sundays from 8:00 to 10:00 am, mid-June through mid-October. Each day is limited to 15 tickets.

$65 per person, or $110 for two with the plus-one bundle (choose when you book).

Plus-one option

U-Pick Ticket

One jar of flowers per ticket · 8:00–10:00 am

Pick a mason-jar bouquet of whatever is blooming that weekend: snapdragons, strawflowers, dahlias and more as the season turns. Coming with someone? Choose the plus-one bundle for two people and two jars, $110.

$65

Good to know before you come

  • The farm is open for U-pick from 8:00 to 10:00 am.
  • Check-in happens at the front of the farm.
  • Please bring a reusable mason jar to pick your flowers.
  • We provide water and sanitized snips for harvesting your flowers.
  • We provide flower food, and we're happy to condition your flowers in our cooler for longer vase life if you'd like.
  • Children and extra guests are welcome to come along with a ticket holder, but picking is limited to one jar of flowers per ticket purchased.
  • If we close for hail or storms, your ticket rolls over to another open U-pick day.

Pick your U-pick day

Terms & Liability Waiver required. U-pick happens in a working field alive with bees. You'll read and sign our Terms & Liability Waiver when you book, and every adult in your party signs again at check-in.

Flower CSA

Our Community Supported Agriculture subscription brings a fresh, hand-tied bouquet of whatever is blooming on the farm to your door, or to our farm stand for pickup, all season long. You'll see the season change in your vase, from early snapdragons to late dahlias, and your subscription helps a small local farm plan and grow. Every plan is $40 per bouquet.

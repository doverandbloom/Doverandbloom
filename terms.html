2. Inherent risks of a working farm

Dover & Bloom is a working, regenerative flower farm that is deliberately managed as pollinator habitat. That means large numbers of bees, wasps and other insects live and forage here. Visiting involves risks that cannot be removed without changing the nature of the farm. These risks include, but are not limited to:

  • Stings and bites from bees, wasps, hornets, ants, mosquitoes, ticks and spiders, which can cause allergic reactions including anaphylaxis, a potentially life-threatening reaction;
  • Uneven, muddy, wet or slippery ground, including holes, raised beds, irrigation lines, hoses, mulch, stakes, trellis netting and other trip hazards;
  • Sharp tools, including clippers, snips and knives, and the risk of cuts and punctures;
  • Plants that may have thorns or spines, or may cause skin irritation, allergic reactions, or illness if eaten. Do not eat any plant or flower on the farm.
  • Weather and sun, including heat, dehydration, sunburn, lightning, hail, high winds and sudden storms common on the Front Range;
  • Wildlife and animals, including birds, snakes and rodents;
  • Physical activity, including yoga, stretching, bending, kneeling and walking, which can cause strains, sprains or other injury. Yoga participants should consult a doctor before beginning any exercise program and stop if they feel pain or discomfort;
  • Alcohol served at private events by a licensed caterer, and the actions of guests who have been drinking;
  • Farm operations, including equipment, vehicles, water features, compost and soil amendments;
  • Your own actions or failure to follow rules, and the actions of other guests.

You understand these risks and voluntarily choose to accept them, whether or not they are listed above, and you assume full responsibility for any injury, illness, death, loss or damage that results.

7. Farm rules

To keep you, the flowers and the pollinators safe, all guests agree to:

  • Check in on arrival and follow all posted signs and staff directions;
  • Stay on marked paths, and cut only from rows marked as open for U-pick;
  • Wear closed-toe shoes, and bring water, sunscreen and a hat;
  • Move calmly around bees and insects. Do not swat at them, and stay out of marked nesting and habitat areas;
  • Leave pets at home (service animals as defined by the ADA are welcome);
  • No smoking, vaping, drones, or pesticides of any kind on the Property, and no outside alcohol (alcohol at private events may be served only by a licensed caterer, see Section 10);
  • Leave the farm promptly at the end of your session or event.

We may refuse entry to, or ask to leave, anyone who doesn't follow these rules or who behaves unsafely, without a refund.

9. Tickets, weather & refunds

U-pick tickets

  • U-pick runs every other weekend, Saturdays and Sundays, from 8:00 to 10:00 am. Each day is limited to 15 tickets.
  • A U-Pick Ticket ($65) entitles the holder to pick one jar of flowers (in a mason jar you bring) on the date you choose. A Plus-One Bundle ($110) is two tickets for the same date, counts as two of the day's 15 tickets, and entitles you to two jars; the bundle price applies only when both are purchased together.
  • Children and extra guests may accompany a ticket holder, but picking is limited to one jar of flowers for each ticket purchased, no matter how many people are in your party. Guests who wish to pick their own jar need their own ticket. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times, and every adult guest, ticketed or not, must sign the waiver at check-in.
  • Please check in at the front of the farm when you arrive. U-pick ends promptly at 10:00 am.
  • Bloom availability changes daily. We can't guarantee particular varieties or colors on any date.
  • Tickets are non-refundable but may be rescheduled once with at least 48 hours' notice, subject to availability. Missed sessions (no-shows) cannot be refunded or rescheduled.

Events & classes

  • Flower Happy Hour, Friday Flower Date Night (a couple counts as two participants), Brunch & Blooms, Bloom & Breathe yoga, and our bouquet making, wreath making, Christmas wreath, pumpkin decorating and DIY suncatcher classes are each limited to 20 participants per date. A Plus-One ticket admits two people and counts as two participants.
  • Brunch & Blooms guests must tell us about food allergies at least 3 days before the event. We can't guarantee an allergen-free kitchen.
  • Event and class tickets are non-refundable, but you may transfer your ticket to another person by emailing us before the event. The new guest must accept these Terms.
  • If we cancel an event or class, you'll get a full refund or a credit toward a future date, whichever you choose.
  • Webinar tickets include access to the recording, which is for your personal use only and may not be shared or redistributed.

Weather & closures

  • Activities generally happen rain or shine. We may delay, shorten or close for lightning, hail, high winds, extreme heat, smoke, or anything else that makes the farm unsafe.
  • If we close before or during your session, your ticket rolls over to another open date of your choice. Rollover is the remedy for weather closures; we don't issue cash refunds for weather.
  • A frost may end the U-pick season early. Unused dated tickets will be refunded or credited if that happens.

10. Private events & bloom bar rentals

Private events on the farm

  • Private events are priced by custom quote based on your guest count, date, package and extras. A date is confirmed only after you accept our written quote and we receive a 50% non-refundable deposit. The balance is due 14 days before the event.
  • The person booking (the "Host") agrees to these Terms on behalf of the event and is responsible for the conduct of all guests and for any damage to the Property, plants or equipment beyond normal wear.
  • Every adult guest must sign our Terms & Liability Waiver on arrival, and a parent or guardian must sign for each minor. Guests who do not sign may not enter the growing areas.
  • The Host must tell us in advance about any guest with a known bee- or insect-sting allergy.
  • Private events are limited to 50 guests, including children. Guest count may not exceed the package booked. Events end at the scheduled time, and setup and cleanup are included in the booked hours.
  • Food may be brought in by the Host or a caterer. Alcohol may be served only by a licensed and insured caterer or bartender. No glitter, confetti, balloons released outdoors, open flames or amplified music without our written approval.
  • If you cancel 30 or more days before the event, you may move your deposit to another date in the same season once. Cancellations made within 30 days forfeit the deposit.
  • If we cancel for weather or unsafe conditions, you may choose another open date or receive a full refund of amounts paid.

Bloom bar rentals

  • Bloom bar packages do not include staff. A florist to help your guests can be added for $125 per hour, shown in your quote. A $100 deposit holds your date and is applied to the rental price. The balance is due 7 days before the event.
  • Delivery, setup and pickup are free within 5 miles of the farm. For events farther away, a delivery surcharge of $5 per mile beyond the first 5 miles is added to your rental. We measure driving distance from the farm and include the surcharge in your quote before you pay the deposit.
  • You agree to provide a level, shaded or covered spot near the entrance with clear access for our cart. The cart, buckets, signage and tools remain our property, and you are responsible for loss or damage during your event.
  • Flowers vary with the season and weather. We'll match your color palette as closely as we can, and we may substitute similar varieties.
  • Cancellations 14 or more days before the event receive a refund of amounts paid minus the deposit. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable.

11. Online purchases & store policies

Secure payments

All payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor, on Stripe's secure hosted checkout page. Your card details are encrypted and sent directly to Stripe. Dover & Bloom never sees or stores your full card number. All prices are in US dollars, and applicable sales tax may be added at checkout.

Dahlia tubers

  • Tubers ship in spring when it is safe to plant (typically April–May) to US addresses only, or can be picked up at the farm by arrangement.
  • Each tuber is guaranteed to arrive with at least one visible eye. Named varieties are guaranteed true to their label to the best of our knowledge. Mystery Tubers are unidentified, so their variety and color can't be guaranteed. If a tuber arrives damaged or rotten, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or store credit.
  • Because growing conditions are out of our control, we can't guarantee growth or bloom once tubers are planted.
  • Some states restrict imports of plant material. It is your responsibility to know your state's rules.

Flower CSA subscriptions

  • Flower CSA plans are billed automatically by Stripe at $40 per bouquet: weekly, every two weeks or monthly, depending on your plan. Nothing is charged when you sign up; your first charge is on the first delivery day of the season.
  • Your subscription ends automatically at the end of the season. You may cancel any time before your next charge by emailing us, and you will not be charged again. Charges for deliveries already made are non-refundable.
  • Bouquets are delivered on Fridays to addresses within 5 miles of the farm. Subscribers outside that area pick up at the farm on Fridays, 3:00–6:00 pm; bouquets not picked up by 6:00 pm can't be held or refunded. If your delivery address is outside our area, we'll contact you to switch to pickup. If no one is available for a delivery, we'll leave your bouquet in a shaded spot. Missed deliveries due to an incorrect address can't be refunded. To skip a delivery, email us by the Wednesday before.
  • Bouquet contents vary with the season and weather. If hail or a hard freeze prevents a delivery, we'll skip that charge or add extra stems to a later bouquet.

Bulk flower buckets

  • Bulk buckets are pre-ordered for farm pickup on the Friday you choose, from 3:00 to 6:00 pm, and must be ordered at least 14 days ahead.
  • You choose a color palette (bright & bold, or neutral, pastel & blush) when ordering. Stem counts are approximate. We may substitute varieties within your chosen palette and of similar quality when weather, hail or heat affects what's blooming.
  • Orders may be moved to another pickup Friday with at least 7 days' notice. Buckets not picked up by the end of the pickup window are forfeited without refund.
  • Cut flowers are perishable and are guaranteed fresh at pickup only.

On-demand video classes

  • When you buy an on-demand class, you can stream or download it from your order confirmation page any time. Please bookmark that page. If you lose the link, email us with the address you used to order and we'll resend it.
  • Classes are licensed for your personal, non-commercial use. Please don't share, upload, resell or show them publicly.
  • Because classes can be downloaded right away, all sales of on-demand classes are final. If a file won't play or download, email us and we'll fix it.

Plants & seeds

  • Live plants are farm pickup only on the dates we announce. Plants not picked up within 14 days of the pickup window are forfeited without refund.
  • Seed packets are guaranteed to arrive in good condition. Germination depends on growing conditions and can't be guaranteed.

Returns

Because our goods are living plants and dated tickets, all sales are final except as described above. If something isn't right, please email us. We're a family farm and we'll do our best to make it right.

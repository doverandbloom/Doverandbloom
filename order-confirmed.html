We're a small urban farm on a residential street. Please park on the street, keep driveways clear and check in at the front.

    Coming to the farm?

    • Bring your email receipt (on your phone is fine) and arrive 10 minutes early to check in.
    • Event guests: your receipt lists the date, time and what to bring. Bloom & Breathe yoga starts at 8:00 am sharp, so bring your own mat.
    • U-pick guests: the farm is open from 8:00 to 10:00 am, and check-in happens at the front. Please bring a reusable mason jar to pick your flowers. We provide water and sanitized snips for harvesting. Children and extra guests are welcome to come along, but picking is limited to one jar of flowers per ticket. We provide flower food, and we're happy to condition your flowers in our cooler for longer vase life if you'd like.
    • Every adult in your party will sign our Terms & Liability Waiver at check-in.
    • Wear closed-toe shoes and bring water, sunscreen and a hat. If anyone has a sting allergy, bring their epinephrine.

    Questions or need to reschedule? Email andrea@doverandbloom.com.

Our Story

Two generations, one garden

How a father, a daughter and a backyard full of dahlias became a small urban farm for flowers and the creatures that need them.

Who we are

A family farm for flowers, people and pollinators

Dover & Bloom is a family-owned regenerative flower farm in Lakewood, Colorado, where a passion for sustainable agriculture, community connection and ecological stewardship come together.

Run by the father–daughter team of Michael and Andrea, we grow beautiful seasonal flowers and native, pollinator-friendly plants using regenerative practices that nurture soil health, support biodiversity and strengthen our local ecosystem.

Grown for this place

At the heart of Dover & Bloom is a commitment to flowers that are both environmentally responsible and deeply connected to place. By favoring native and pollinator-supporting species, we create vibrant habitat for bees, butterflies and other beneficial insects, while growing high-quality blooms for our community.

Through regenerative growing, community-centered programming and a dedication to environmental stewardship, we're building a sustainable model for local flower production, and inviting everyone to reconnect with nature, support pollinators and celebrate the beauty of seasonal blooms.

In Andrea’s words

How Dover & Bloom came to be

Gardening is part of who my dad is

Ever since I can remember, my dad, Michael, has been a gardener, from a small raised bed in the middle of downtown Denver to the house my parents live in today. They bought this property because it gave Dad the chance to grow and sell echinacea on a large scale. The moral of the story: gardening has always been a big part of our lives. It’s part of my father’s makeup.

My winding road to the garden

I took a more winding road. I earned a degree in engineering and a PhD in geology, all so I could spend my days outdoors, in nature. The joke was on me: I spend 98% of my days behind a computer, stuck inside.

When COVID hit, I needed an outlet, so I started small herb and flower gardens at our house in Houston. Once our family moved back to Colorado, I planted as many flower gardens as I could, to enjoy the flowers and to provide habitat and food for native pollinators, which are in massive decline. With each of my maternity leaves, the number of gardens grew.

That’s when I started to truly understand my dad’s love of gardening. It’s a way to spend time outdoors, with my hands in the dirt, appreciating the moment.

And so Dover & Bloom was born

With the birth of my second child, the dream of Dover & Bloom was born, and realized. I knew I was at a place in my life where I wanted to spend quality time with my family, both my kids and my parents, and to grow something I was proud of: something that supports our ever-changing climate and the pollinators that depend on it, and that helps me hold on to the small things in life, those moments that are gone as fast as they arrived.

So we’re finally growing at a large scale on my parents’ property, as a way to connect with nature and with our community. Dover & Bloom is a place where everyone is welcome and community is at our core. Hence our core values: Grow. Gather. Create.

Come join us!

Andrea

What We Believe

Growing with the land, not against it

Regenerative farming means every season should leave the soil, the water and the wildlife a little better off.

I

Feed the soil first

We never till. Compost, mulch and cover crops build the living soil that grows strong, long-lasting stems.

II

Keep roots in the ground

Something is always growing in our beds, which protects Colorado's clay from erosion and keeps soil life fed through winter.

III

Plant for pollinators

Native perennials, flowering shrubs and bare-ground nesting areas give bees, butterflies and hummingbirds food and shelter all season.

IV

No synthetic chemicals

No synthetic pesticides, herbicides or fertilizers. We rely on healthy soil, diverse plantings and beneficial insects instead.

V

Water wisely

Drip irrigation, deep mulch and plants suited to our semi-arid climate help us make every drop count.

VI

Grow community

Through U-pick days, classes and open farm events, we want our neighbors to feel at home in the garden.

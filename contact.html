Quick answers

Can I visit the farm without a ticket?

We're a working farm on a residential street, so visits are by U-pick ticket, event ticket or appointment only. That keeps things calm for our neighbors and the bees.

Do you deliver flowers?

Bulk buckets are farm pickup on Fridays. Flower CSA bouquets are delivered on Fridays within 5 miles of the farm, or picked up at the farm. Bloom bar rentals include free delivery and setup within 5 miles of the farm, with a $5-per-mile surcharge beyond that. Dahlia tubers ship in spring.

How far ahead should I book a private event?

Weekend dates in June and September go fast, so we suggest 6–8 weeks ahead. We confirm requests within two business days.

Do you sell to florists?

Yes. We offer wholesale stems to local florists and designers during the season. Choose “Wholesale for florists” above and we'll send our availability list.

Is the farm accessible?

Our main paths are packed gravel and mostly flat, but the growing rows are uneven. Let us know what you need and we'll do our best to help.

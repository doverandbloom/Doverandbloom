Grow · Gather · Create

How we farm

Every stem we cut comes from beds that also feed bees, butterflies and birds.

Regenerative growing

No-till beds, compost made on site, cover crops and living roots year-round. No synthetic pesticides, ever. We are building soil, not just using it.

Pollinator habitat

Native perennials, flowering hedgerows and undisturbed nesting ground are woven between the cutting rows, so native bees and other pollinators have food from spring to frost.

Two generations

Andrea and her dad, Michael, 38 years apart, sharing one garden. Decades of growing know-how meet fresh ideas, and our neighbors are always welcome.

Our Story

A family garden that grew up

For as long as Andrea can remember, her dad, Michael, has been a gardener, from a raised bed in downtown Denver to growing echinacea on the property where we farm today. Andrea found her own winding way back to the garden, and with the birth of her second child, Dover & Bloom was born.

Today, Andrea and Michael tend a small urban farm in Lakewood. It's part cutting garden, part pollinator sanctuary, and part classroom for anyone who wants to grow something beautiful.
